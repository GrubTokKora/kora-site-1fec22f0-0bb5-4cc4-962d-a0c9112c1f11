Your Home Routine

A Daily Cleansing Protocol That Works

After every facial, Harpreet shares a personalised daily cleansing protocol so your skin continues to improve between visits. Here are the core steps she recommends for most skin types.

01

Gentle Double Cleanse

Begin with an oil-based cleanser to dissolve makeup, SPF, and excess sebum, then follow with a water-based cleanser suited to your skin type. This two-step approach removes impurities without stripping the skin's natural barrier.

02

Targeted Toning

A toner balances the skin's pH after cleansing and prepares it to absorb the active ingredients in the next steps. Harpreet selects alcohol-free, skin-appropriate formulas that calm rather than irritate.

03

Active Serum Application

Serums deliver concentrated actives — antioxidants, peptides, brighteners — directly where the skin needs them most. Your serum is chosen based on your skin assessment, not a generic formula.

04

Moisturiser & Barrier Support

A moisturiser locks in hydration and supports the skin's protective barrier. Even oily skin needs moisture — the right formula prevents the overproduction of sebum that leads to breakouts.

05

Daily SPF (Morning)

Sun protection is the single most important step for long-term skin health. Harpreet recommends a broad-spectrum SPF every morning — even on cloudy days — to protect against premature ageing and hyperpigmentation.

06

Weekly Exfoliation

Gentle exfoliation one to two times per week removes dead skin cells, prevents congestion, and keeps the skin's surface smooth and receptive to treatment products. Harpreet advises on the right exfoliant for your skin to avoid over-stripping.
