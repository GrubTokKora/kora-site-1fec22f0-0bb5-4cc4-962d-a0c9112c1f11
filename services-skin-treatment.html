Healthy Skin Is a Lifestyle

Professional Skin Care That Goes Deeper

At Veda Healing Spa in Fairfield, professional skin treatments are never one-size-fits-all. Founder and licensed esthetician Harpreet Kaur brings over 20 years of experience — rooted in Ayurvedic tradition and sharpened in medical spa settings — to every session. Whether your skin needs a thorough exfoliation, intensive hydration, a corrective peel, or a restorative Kansa Wand facial, Harpreet begins with an honest assessment of where your skin is and builds a plan tailored to your unique goals. Her guiding belief: "I strongly believe the Ayurveda principle that healthy skin should be a lifestyle."

What We Offer

Skin Treatment Services

Each treatment is customized to your skin type and wellness goals. Harpreet selects the techniques and products that are right for you — every time.

Signature Service

Customized Facial with Energy-Point Massage

Harpreet's signature treatment combines professional skincare with massage on vital facial energy points — balancing your skin while delivering deep relaxation. Every step, from cleansing to masking, is chosen for your skin type on the day of your visit.

Exfoliation

Microdermabrasion & Dermaplaning

These professional exfoliation treatments remove dead skin cells and surface build-up to reveal smoother, brighter skin underneath. Microdermabrasion uses controlled abrasion to resurface the skin, while dermaplaning gently removes vellus hair and debris for an instantly refined complexion.

Corrective Treatment

Chemical Peels

Harpreet gained extensive expertise in chemical peels working alongside physicians in medical spa settings. Peels are selected and applied based on your skin's needs, targeting concerns such as uneven tone, fine lines, congestion, and acne — with clinical results and minimal downtime.

Ayurvedic Technique

Kansa Wand Facial

One of the safest, most effective, and ancient Ayurvedic techniques, the Kansa Wand facial has helped many of Harpreet's clients achieve visible results. Harpreet has traveled throughout India to learn from Ayurveda experts and bring this restorative practice to her clients in Fairfield.

Hydration & Restoration

Deep Hydration Therapy

Targeted hydration treatments replenish moisture at every layer of the skin. Harpreet selects serums, masks, and techniques appropriate for your skin's current state — whether it's dehydrated, sensitized, or simply in need of a restorative reset — leaving skin supple, plump, and luminous.

Holistic Wellness

Energy Healing Sessions

Harpreet believes that energy healing and skin care go hand in hand — skin treatment is just one step to achieving healthy skin. Complimentary 20-minute energy healing sessions are available so you can explore the power of holistic wellness alongside your skincare journey.

Licensed Esthetician · 20+ Years Experience

Harpreet Kaur's Approach to Skin Health

Harpreet was born and raised in Punjab, India, and developed a passion for skincare from an early age — growing up incorporating Ayurvedic skincare regimes before studying at Shahnaz Husain College of Beauty, where she built a strong foundation in Ayurvedic techniques.

After coming to the United States over 15 years ago, she gained experience in medical spas working alongside physicians, becoming an expert in chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and dermaplaning. She has always been determined to produce clinical results alongside the best relaxation techniques.

"I truly believe that energy healing and skin care go hand in hand because skin treatment is just one step to achieving healthy skin."

What clients appreciate most is Harpreet's honest assessment of where their skin is — and a clear, realistic plan for improvement that extends beyond the treatment room.
