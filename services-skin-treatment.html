Our Skin Therapies

Exfoliation, Hydration & Restorative Skin Treatments

Microdermabrasion

A controlled mechanical exfoliation that polishes away dead skin cells, smooths uneven texture, and stimulates collagen production. Microdermabrasion is ideal for dull, congested, or sun-damaged skin and allows active serums to penetrate far more effectively after treatment. Harpreet trained in medical spa settings alongside physicians, ensuring safe, clinical-grade results.

Dermaplaning

A precise manual exfoliation technique that removes the outermost layer of dead skin cells and vellus hair (peach fuzz) using a sterile surgical blade. The result is an immediately smoother, brighter complexion with improved product absorption. Dermaplaning is safe for most skin types and leaves skin looking luminous with no downtime.

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels use carefully selected acids to resurface the skin, accelerate cell turnover, and address uneven tone, hyperpigmentation, fine lines, and congestion. Harpreet selects the appropriate peel strength and formulation for your skin type, drawing on her extensive experience working alongside physicians in medical spa environments to deliver safe, measurable results.

Deep Hydration Treatment

Dehydrated skin — even oily skin — benefits enormously from targeted hydration therapy. This treatment layers moisture-binding ingredients, barrier-supporting actives, and nourishing masks to restore the skin's water balance, plump fine lines, and calm inflammation. Harpreet selects formulations based on your skin assessment, not a generic protocol.

Restorative Skin Therapy

For skin that has been compromised by stress, environmental damage, post-procedure sensitivity, or chronic conditions, restorative therapy focuses on rebuilding the skin's natural barrier, reducing redness, and encouraging healthy cell renewal. Harpreet combines Ayurvedic calming techniques with clinical actives to bring stressed skin back into balance.

Kansa Wand Restorative Facial

One of the most ancient and effective Ayurvedic restorative treatments available, the Kansa Wand works on vital facial energy points to reduce puffiness, improve circulation, and restore the skin's natural vitality. Harpreet has traveled throughout India to learn this technique from Ayurveda experts and brings that authentic knowledge to every session.

Your Treatment Journey

How Each Skin Treatment Is Structured

Every skin treatment at Veda Healing Spa follows a thoughtful sequence designed to maximise results while respecting your skin's current condition. Here is what you can expect from a typical session.

01

Skin Assessment

Before any treatment begins, Harpreet conducts a thorough assessment of your skin — examining texture, tone, hydration levels, sensitivity, and any active concerns. This ensures the treatment is built around your skin's actual needs on that day, not a generic template.

02

Cleansing & Preparation

The skin is thoroughly cleansed to remove impurities, makeup, and excess oil. This step prepares the surface to receive active ingredients and ensures that exfoliation and hydration treatments can work at their full depth without interference.

03

Targeted Exfoliation

Depending on your skin type and goals, Harpreet applies the appropriate exfoliation method — mechanical (microdermabrasion or dermaplaning) or chemical (a carefully selected peel). This step removes dead cells, unclogs pores, and creates a fresh canvas for the treatments that follow.

04

Active Treatment & Hydration

Serums, masks, and targeted actives are applied to address your specific concerns — whether that is deep hydration, brightening, barrier repair, or anti-ageing. Harpreet layers treatments strategically so each ingredient can work at its optimal depth and concentration.

05

Restorative Massage & Energy Work

Facial massage on vital energy points promotes circulation, reduces tension, and supports lymphatic drainage — accelerating the skin's natural healing response. For clients who benefit from it, Harpreet incorporates Kansa Wand or Ayurvedic energy techniques at this stage.

06

Home Care Guidance

Every treatment concludes with personalised advice on how to maintain and extend your results at home — the right products, the right sequence, and the right frequency. Harpreet's clients consistently credit this guidance as the reason their skin continues to improve between visits.

Common Questions

Skin Treatment FAQ

What is the difference between exfoliation treatments?

Microdermabrasion uses a mechanical device to physically buff away dead skin cells, while dermaplaning uses a sterile blade to remove both dead cells and vellus hair. Chemical peels use acids to dissolve the bonds between dead cells and accelerate cell turnover. Harpreet will recommend the right method — or a combination — based on your skin type, concerns, and goals during your assessment.

Is there any downtime after a skin treatment?

Most treatments at Veda Healing Spa involve little to no downtime. Dermaplaning and microdermabrasion typically leave the skin looking immediately brighter with minimal redness. Superficial chemical peels may cause mild flaking for a few days. Harpreet will advise you on exactly what to expect and how to care for your skin in the days following your treatment.

How often should I have a professional skin treatment?

For most clients, a professional skin treatment every four to six weeks aligns with the skin's natural cell turnover cycle and delivers the best cumulative results. Harpreet will recommend a personalised treatment schedule based on your skin's condition and your goals — whether that is monthly maintenance or a more intensive course of treatments to address a specific concern.

Are these treatments suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Every treatment at Veda Healing Spa is fully customised to your skin's needs. Harpreet has extensive experience working with sensitive, reactive, and compromised skin and will always select the gentlest effective approach for your skin type. She discusses any concerns with you before beginning and adjusts the treatment in real time based on your skin's response.
