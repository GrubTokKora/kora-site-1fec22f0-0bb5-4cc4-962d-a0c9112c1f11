Customized Facials
Personalized to Your Skin · Every Single Visit
  • Signature Veda Facial
    A fully customized facial tailored to your skin type and concerns — includes cleansing, exfoliation, extractions, mask, and vital-point facial massage for deep relaxation and healthy skin
  • Radiance Glow Facial
    Brightening treatment targeting dullness, uneven tone, and pigmentation — leaves skin luminous and refreshed
  • Anti-Aging Facial
    Firming and lifting treatment focused on fine lines, loss of elasticity, and age spots — using targeted serums and massage techniques
  • Acne Clarifying Facial
    Deep-cleansing treatment for breakout-prone skin — calms inflammation, unclogs pores, and balances oil production
  • Sensitive Skin Facial
    Gentle, soothing treatment for reactive or rosacea-prone skin — reduces redness and restores the skin barrier
Peels & Exfoliation
Resurfacing · Renewing · Results-Driven
  • Enzyme Peel
    Gentle fruit-enzyme exfoliation — ideal for sensitive skin, brightens and smooths without downtime
  • AHA/BHA Chemical Peel
    Alpha and beta hydroxy acid blend targeting acne, congestion, and uneven texture — customized to your skin's tolerance
  • Brightening Peel
    Targets hyperpigmentation, sun damage, and melasma — reveals a more even, radiant complexion over a series of treatments
  • Retinol Resurfacing Peel
    Stimulates cell turnover and collagen production — addresses fine lines, texture, and dullness for a youthful glow
Energy Healing
Restore Balance · Calm the Mind · Revive the Skin
  • Vital-Point Facial Energy Massage
    Targeted massage on key facial energy points — promotes lymphatic drainage, reduces tension, and supports healthy, glowing skin from within
  • Reiki-Infused Facial
    A deeply restorative treatment combining traditional facial techniques with Reiki energy healing — for clients seeking both skin results and profound relaxation
  • Chakra Balancing Skin Ritual
    Holistic session aligning energy centers while nourishing the skin — includes breathwork, intention setting, and a customized facial treatment
Ayurvedic Treatments
Ancient Wisdom · Modern Results
  • Dosha-Balancing Facial
    Rooted in Ayurvedic principles, this treatment is customized to your unique dosha (Vata, Pitta, or Kapha) — using botanical ingredients and techniques to restore harmony to your skin
  • Herbal Compress Treatment
    Warm herbal compresses infused with Ayurvedic botanicals — deeply relaxing, detoxifying, and nourishing for all skin types
  • Abhyanga Face & Scalp Ritual
    Traditional Ayurvedic warm oil massage for the face and scalp — stimulates circulation, relieves stress, and deeply conditions skin and hair
  • Ubtan Brightening Mask
    A traditional Ayurvedic blend of turmeric, chickpea flour, and botanicals — brightens, exfoliates, and evens skin tone naturally
Add-Ons & Enhancements
Elevate Any Treatment
  • LED Light Therapy
    Red or blue light therapy to boost collagen, reduce inflammation, and target acne-causing bacteria
  • Gua Sha Facial Sculpting
    Traditional stone tool technique to lift, contour, and depuff the face — promotes circulation and lymphatic flow
  • Eye Treatment
    Targeted treatment for dark circles, puffiness, and fine lines around the delicate eye area
  • Lip Treatment
    Exfoliating and hydrating treatment to smooth, plump, and nourish the lips
  • Personalized Skin Care Consultation
    One-on-one session with Harpreet to build your at-home regimen — product recommendations tailored to your skin concerns and lifestyle
