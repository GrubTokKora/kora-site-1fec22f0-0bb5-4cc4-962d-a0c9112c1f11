About Veda Healing Spa

With over 20+ years of experience as a licensed esthetician, Harpreet Kaur believes that everyone deserves to feel confident within their skin. Harpreet’s mission has always been to bring innovative and research-driven treatments to her clients alongside her expertise in Ayurvedic techniques.

“I strongly believe the Ayurveda principle that healthy skin should be a lifestyle.”

Harpreet was born and raised in Punjab, India and was ambitious to learn about skincare at a young age. She grew up incorporating Ayurvedic skincare regimes, then went on to study the roots of skincare at Shahnaz Husain College of Beauty and built a strong foundation in Ayurvedic techniques.

On coming to the United States over 15 years ago, Harpreet gained experience working in various spa settings including medical spas with physicians. She became an expert at chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and dermaplaning, and also shared her expertise in Ayurveda techniques in various spas. Harpreet has always been determined to produce clinical results and bring the best relaxation techniques to her clients. One of the safest, most effective, and ancient Ayurvedic techniques that has helped many of her clients is the Kansa Wand facial. She has traveled many parts of India to learn from Ayurveda experts and bring that knowledge to her clients.

“I truly believe that energy healing and skin care go hand in hand because skin treatment is just one step to achieving healthy skin.”

Covid-19 Protocol

Your safety is my priority.

Clients will text Harpreet upon arrival. Harpreet will greet you with mask and face shield. Upon walking into Phenix Suites, clients are required for sterilize their hands with the touchless hand sanitizing system. Everyone is required to wear a mask. Harpreet Kaur, owner and esthetician will check the client's temperature. The client will complete a covid consent form. Please do not bring water bottles. If the client would like water, Harpreet will provide bottled water to her clients.

During the treatment:
➡️ The esthetician, Harpreet will wear a mask, face shield and gloves.
➡️ The client will be given a ziplock to place their mask and be given a new mask upon completing their treatment.
➡️ In between each treatment, the room is sterilized per state regulations.

➡️ Bedsheets, blankets, equipment, any item used during a session are washed and sterilized after every session per state regulations.

Harpreet takes the safety of her clients very seriously and strictly abides by the state regulations for all parts of the treatment. Please feel free to contact Veda Healing Spa with any additional question
