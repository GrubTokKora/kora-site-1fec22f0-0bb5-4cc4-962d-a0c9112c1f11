About Veda Healing Spa

With over 20+ years of experience as a licensed esthetician, Harpreet Kaur believes that everyone deserves to feel confident within their skin. Harpreet’s mission has always been to bring innovative and research-driven treatments to her clients alongside her expertise in Ayurvedic techniques.

“I strongly believe the Ayurveda principle that healthy skin should be a lifestyle.”

Harpreet was born and raised in Punjab, India and was ambitious to learn about skincare at a young age. She grew up incorporating Ayurvedic skincare regimes, then went on to study the roots of skincare at Shahnaz Husain College of Beauty and built a strong foundation in Ayurvedic techniques.

On coming to the United States over 15 years ago, Harpreet gained experience working in various spa settings including medical spas with physicians. She became an expert at chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and dermaplaning, and also shared her expertise in Ayurveda techniques in various spas. Harpreet has always been determined to produce clinical results and bring the best relaxation techniques to her clients. One of the safest, most effective, and ancient Ayurvedic techniques that has helped many of her clients is the Kansa Wand facial. She has traveled many parts of India to learn from Ayurveda experts and bring that knowledge to her clients.

“I truly believe that energy healing and skin care go hand in hand because skin treatment is just one step to achieving healthy skin.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit to Veda Healing Spa.

What services do you offer?

Veda Healing Spa specializes in customized facials with massage on vital facial energy points, Kansa Wand facials, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, dermaplaning, and energy healing sessions. Every treatment is tailored to your unique skin type and wellness goals by founder and licensed esthetician Harpreet Kaur. View the full service menu →

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online anytime through our booking page at vedahealingspa.glossgenius.com. You may also call us at 203-297-7000 or email vedahealingspa@gmail.com. We are open Monday–Saturday, 11:30 am–6 pm by appointment only. Accommodations outside listed hours can be arranged — just reach out!

What should I wear or bring to my appointment?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. For facial treatments, we recommend arriving with a clean face if possible, though cleansing is part of every service. Please avoid wearing heavy jewelry around the neck and face. We provide everything you need for your treatment — just bring yourself and arrive a few minutes early to relax and settle in.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! Veda Healing Spa gift cards are the perfect way to share the gift of relaxation and healthy skin with someone you love. You can purchase an e-gift card instantly online. Buy a gift card →

Is parking available?

Yes, parking is available near our location at 69 Sherman St W, Suite 2S, Fairfield, CT 06824. If you have any trouble finding us, please don't hesitate to call us at 203-297-7000 and we'll be happy to help guide you.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday through Saturday, 11:30 am–6:00 pm. We are closed on Sundays. All visits are by appointment only. If you need an appointment outside of our regular hours, please contact us — we are happy to accommodate when possible.

Is Veda Healing Spa right for sensitive skin?

Absolutely. Every treatment at Veda Healing Spa is fully customized to your skin's needs. Harpreet takes time to assess your skin before every session and selects products and techniques that are safe and effective for your skin type — including sensitive, reactive, or acne-prone skin. She will always discuss any concerns with you before beginning.
