Say Goodbye to Oily Skin: Professional Skincare Treatments

January 19, 2023

Oily skin can be a frustrating and challenging skin type to deal with. It is caused by overactive sebaceous glands, which produce too much oil, leading to clogged pores, blackheads, and acne. However, with the right skincare routine and professional treatments, it is possible to manage and control oily skin.

One of the most effective professional skincare treatments for oily skin is a chemical peel. Chemical peels use a combination of acids to exfoliate and resurface the skin, helping to unclog pores and reduce excess oil production. A light or medium-depth peel can be performed to address specific concerns such as acne and hyperpigmentation, or a deeper peel can be done for more severe cases.

Another effective treatment for oily skin is a facial with extractions. During this treatment, an aesthetician will use a gentle extraction method to remove blackheads and whiteheads from the skin. This can help to unclog pores and reduce the appearance of acne.

Regular facials and microdermabrasion also work great for oily skin, as they help to exfoliate dead skin cells, unclog pores, and control oil production. They also aid in deep cleaning the skin, which can help to prevent breakouts.

It is important to note that professional treatments should be used in conjunction with a consistent, daily skincare routine that includes products specifically formulated for oily skin, such as non-comedogenic moisturizers and oil-free sunscreens.
