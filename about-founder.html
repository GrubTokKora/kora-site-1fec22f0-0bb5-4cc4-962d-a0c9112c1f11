Founder & Owner

Harpreet Kaur

My mission is to spread a healing and positive touch as an esthetician and an energy healer. I grew up in India with Ayurvedic traditions and have appreciated the benefits of holistic beauty since childhood and into my esthetician training. I loved being a spa owner in India and helping people achieve a healthy skin lifestyle.

Unfortunately, we all go through difficult times in life. My father's passing was a tough time for me, one that I struggled with bouncing back to my thriving career, my family, and just my daily life. As I found myself lost, I dedicated years to learning meditation and healing in ashrams in India and started my healing journey. The power of cleansing one's aura and redirecting your thoughts; hence your energy changed my life. I am grateful that I got to learn these beautiful healing tools from powerful healers and I wanted to incorporate them into every one of my skin treatments.

Coming to the US over 20 years ago, I focused more on gaining experience as a licensed esthetician, still keeping my healing skills in mind. I worked in a medical spa with plastic surgeons and became an expert in chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and derma-planing. Now, with over 20 years of experience as a licensed esthetician and energy healer, I wanted to start another journey of opening Veda Healing Spa. Veda means knowledge in Sanskrit, and I believe that knowledge is healing. I combined my two passions, and have enjoyed helping my clients achieve unconditional love for themselves.
